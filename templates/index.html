2D Discrete Wavelet Transform (DWT) Decomposition

Slide 3 & 4 Architecture: Decomposes carrier video frame into 4 frequency subbands ($LL, LH, HL, HH$) for imperceptible data embedding.

4-Quadrant 2D Haar Wavelet Decomposition Active Carrier Frame
LL Approximation Sub-Band (Low-Low)

Contains the fundamental average illumination and coarse structure of the frame. Kept clean to prevent visual distortion.

HL Vertical Detail Sub-Band (High-Low)

Captures vertical edges and sharp boundaries. Ideal for steganographic embedding masked by Human Visual System (HVS).

LH Horizontal Detail Sub-Band (Low-High)

Captures horizontal texture variations and fine lines. Used as carrier sub-band for encrypted bitstream.

HH Diagonal Detail Sub-Band (High-High)

Contains high-frequency diagonal noise and texture corners where bit modifications produce zero noticeable visual artifacts.
