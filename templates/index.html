           Technical Architecture
        

        
Steganographic Protocol & Cryptographic Integrity

        

          STENOVISION AI combines transform-domain 2D Discrete Wavelet Decomposition with spatial least significant bit substitution and 32-bit CRC checksum framing to guarantee maximum security, high payload throughput, and zero perceptual degradation.
        

      


      

        

          

          
1. 2D Haar Wavelet Decomposition

          
Each video frame matrix is transformed into four orthogonal subbands: $LL$ (Approximation), $LH$ (Horizontal edges), $HL$ (Vertical edges), and $HH$ (Diagonal texture noise). Secret bits are embedded exclusively into high-frequency details where Human Visual System (HVS) contrast sensitivity is minimal.

        


        

          

          
2. Structured CRC32 Packet Framing

          
Payloads are framed into standardized packets: [4B Magic Header] + [4B Length] + [4B CRC32] + [Payload]. This ensures instantaneous auto-detection by receivers and guarantees 100% losslessly verified data recovery.

        


        

          

          
3. Imperceptible Signal Quality

          
Achieves exceptional quality benchmarks with an Average PSNR > 75 dB (anything above 40 dB is completely invisible to human eyes) and a Structural Similarity Index (SSIM) of 0.999999.

        


        

          

          
4. Multi-Format Carrier Transmission

          
Supports exporting playable WebM video streams, standalone Lossless .stego carrier packages (immune to OS/browser video codec re-compression), and single carrier PNG frames.
