1. Adaptive Frame Selection (AFS)

          
The system evaluates each video frame matrix for spatial texture variance (Laplacian edge complexity) and inter-frame motion dynamics. Frames with rich texture and dynamic motion are automatically prioritized to maximize Human Visual System (HVS) masking.

        


        

          

          
2. Spatial Least Significant Bit (LSB) Substitution

          
Secret payload bits are sequentially substituted into the least significant bit (Bit 0) of the Red, Green, and Blue color channels of selected carrier frames, guaranteeing zero visual distortion ($PSNR > 74 \text{ dB}, SSIM > 0.9999$).

        


        

          

          
3. Structured CRC32 Packet Integrity

          
Payloads are framed into standardized packets: [4B Magic Header] + [4B Length] + [4B CRC32] + [Payload]. This ensures instantaneous auto-detection by receivers and guarantees 100% losslessly verified data recovery.

        


        

          

          
4. Lossless Stream & Image Extraction

          
The extractor scans carrier frames, parses packet headers, and validates the 32-bit CRC checksum. If any byte was altered in transit, the corruption is detected immediately.
